🏢 Digital Twin Lite - Energy Optimisation Dashboard

**Digital Twin Lite** is an interactive, web-based dashboard designed to monitor, forecast, and optimise building energy consumption. It provides actionable insights on efficiency, CO₂ emissions, sustainability, and anomaly detection.

---

## **Features**

- Upload building energy CSV files and visualize data.
- Forecast energy usage using AI/ML models.
- Anomaly detection to highlight energy spikes.
- Executive Summary with:
  - Total Energy Consumption
  - Average Efficiency
  - CO₂ Emissions
  - Sustainability Score
  - Optimization Status
- Building Comparison: compare multiple buildings.
- Live IoT Sensor Simulation.
- Role-based dashboard (Admin/User).
- Generate downloadable PDF and Excel reports.
- CO₂ trend and sustainability visualizations.
- # Digital Twin AI Energy Platform

## Features
- Real-time IoT simulation
- AI failure prediction
- Autonomous decision engine
- Smart maintenance scheduling
- Self-learning AI (drift detection)

- Python, Streamlit
- Machine Learning (Random Forest)
- MQTT (IoT)
